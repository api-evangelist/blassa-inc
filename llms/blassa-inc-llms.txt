# Blassa

> AI-powered location intelligence and geocoding built for the Middle East and Africa.

Blassa provides geocoding, address validation, and location APIs designed for the informal addressing systems common across MEA — helping logistics, e-commerce, and delivery companies reduce failed deliveries.
